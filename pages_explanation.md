# CloudGuard AI — Dashboard Pages Guide

CloudGuard AI features an 8-page dashboard designed to provide comprehensive visibility, FinOps cost optimization, and security intelligence for multi-cloud environments. Below is a detailed explanation of what each page does and how it utilizes the AI engines.

---

## 1. Dashboard (`/`)
**Purpose:** The central command center providing a high-level overview of your cloud infrastructure's health, costs, and risks.

**Key Features:**
- **System Health & Risk Gauge:** Displays the real-time AI-calculated risk score (0-100) combining cost, resource, behavior, and abuse factors.
- **Cost Timeline:** A chart tracking daily cloud spend vs. predicted baseline (powered by the Prophet forecasting model).
- **Active Anomalies:** A quick summary of the most critical issues currently detected across your environment.
- **Provider Breakdown:** A visual breakdown of resources and costs across AWS, Azure, and GCP.

---

## 2. Alerts (`/alerts`)
**Purpose:** The primary incident response queue where you view, triage, and action all anomalies detected by the AI models.

**Key Features:**
- **Severity Filtering:** Filter alerts by Critical, High, Medium, or Low severity.
- **Financial Impact:** Shows the estimated waste (in dollars) directly attached to each alert.
- **Root Cause & Remediation:** Expanding an alert reveals the AI-determined root cause and offers one-click remediation actions (e.g., "Stop Instance", "Downsize").
- **Acknowledgement:** Allows operators to acknowledge or dismiss alerts to remove them from the active queue.

---

## 3. Resource Explorer (`/resources`)
**Purpose:** A unified inventory of all your cloud resources across all providers (AWS, Azure, GCP) with real-time status and AI insights attached.

**Key Features:**
- **Cross-Cloud Search:** Search and filter resources regardless of where they are hosted.
- **Anomaly Badges:** Resources are tagged with AI-detected states like "idle_gpu", "orphaned_snapshot", or "normal".
- **Orphaned Infrastructure highlighting:** Automatically surfaces resources that are detached or unused but still costing money (e.g., unattached EBS volumes, zombie load balancers).
- **Resource Details Drawer:** Clicking a resource opens a side panel with detailed metrics, cost per hour, and direct AI recommendations.

---

## 4. Cost Optimization (`/optimization`)
**Purpose:** The FinOps command center powered by the Gemini AI Optimization Agent. It actively looks for ways to save money without impacting performance.

**Key Features:**
- **Savings Dashboard:** Tracks "Realized Savings" (money saved from applied fixes) and "Pending Savings" (potential savings from open recommendations).
- **AI Recommendations:** Lists specific, actionable advice generated by Gemini (e.g., "Downsize g4dn.xlarge to t3.large").
- **Confidence Scores:** Shows the AI's confidence level (0-100%) in its recommendation to help you decide whether to apply it.
- **One-Click Fix:** The "Apply Fix" button triggers the auto-remediation workflow to execute the recommendation.

---

## 5. Billing Explorer (`/billing`)
**Purpose:** Deep-dive financial analysis to understand exactly where your cloud budget is going.

**Key Features:**
- **Cost by Provider & Service:** Doughnut and Bar charts breaking down spend by cloud provider (AWS/Azure/GCP) and specific service (EC2, S3, RDS, etc.).
- **30-Day Cost Timeline:** Visualizes total daily spend against an anomaly-free baseline.
- **Savings Opportunity Summary:** Calculates total potential monthly savings by aggregating all idle and orphaned resources.
- **Top Spenders:** A table highlighting the most expensive individual resources in your environment and their estimated waste proportion.

---

## 6. Security Threats (`/security`)
**Purpose:** The SecOps hub focused strictly on malicious activity and infrastructure abuse.

**Key Features:**
- **Abuse Detection:** Highlights findings from the XGBoost model, such as cryptomining workloads or suspicious GPU usage.
- **Infrastructure Anomalies:** Surfaces findings from the DBSCAN model, such as abnormal API call spikes or sudden cluster launches in unusual regions.
- **Threat Cards:** Displays the specific threat, its risk score, and the time of detection.
- **Investigation Modal:** Provides a detailed breakdown of the threat, the attributed owner, and options to isolate the resource or block the offending IP.

---

## 7. API Activity (`/api-activity`)
**Purpose:** An intelligent, CloudTrail-style audit log for monitoring user and automated API actions across your clouds.

**Key Features:**
- **Attacker Fingerprinting:** If a user exhibits suspicious behavior (e.g., lots of failed actions, sudden instance launches in specific regions), the system generates an "Attacker Fingerprint" panel prioritizing that user.
- **User Breakdown:** Shows which IAM users/roles are performing the most actions and their failure rates.
- **Live Event Log:** A searchable table of all API events (launches, terminations, describe calls) with color-coded badges and highlighting for external IPs.

---

## 8. Settings (`/settings`)
**Purpose:** Configuration panel to tune the CloudGuard AI engine to your specific environment and preferences.

**Key Features:**
- **Data Source Config:** Switch the engine between SYNTHETIC (simulator), CSV (upload historical data), and LIVE (connect real AWS/Azure/GCP accounts).
- **Alert Thresholds:** Sliders to adjust sensitivity for CPU alerts, dollar-value waste alerts, and GPU idle duration.
- **Auto-Remediation Workflow:** Toggle whether the system is allowed to automatically apply AI fixes, and whether it requires human approval first.
- **Display Preferences:** Change currency (USD, EUR, GBP, INR) and dashboard refresh intervals.
